Stub Fill Test

A sandbox form covering every field type the autofill bookmark handles. Nothing here submits anywhere.

How to use:
  1. In the Stub app, save your details and create your Fill Form bookmark (drag it to your bookmarks bar).
  2. Open this page and tap that bookmark.
  3. Watch fields fill and outline green; required fields it can't guess turn amber; a review panel appears at the bottom. It never submits.

Expected: green = filled by Stub, amber = required but left for you (gender, residency, the made-up "promo code", and marketing opt-in should NOT be filled).
